## What the current prices leave for acquisition

Illustration for an Israeli merchant selling in USD to a US buyer at $24.99.
This is **not a choice of target market**. Planning processor cost is 5.19% +
$0.35, inferred from the VAT-inclusive domestic and US surcharge tables;
the standard USD dispute fee used here is $9.44. Account applicability is
unconfirmed. [PayPal Israel merchant fees](https://www.paypal.com/il/business/paypal-business-fees?locale.x=en_IL).

Assumptions: 5% refunds, 0.5% disputes, no goods recovered on refunds, $0.50
support per paid order, no upsells and no additional platform fee. These rates
are assumptions, not results from this business. Shipping below is a sensitivity
input, not a quote. Currency conversion, sales taxes, duties, additional return
costs, subscriptions and fixed costs remain unpriced; any such cost reduces the
amount available. The target column leaves 15% of revenue **before fixed costs**.

| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $13.78 | $0 | $7.64 | $3.89 |
| $13.78 | $5 | $2.64 | −$1.11 |
| $13.78 | $8 | −$0.36 | −$4.11 |
| $14.99 | $0 | $6.43 | $2.68 |
| $14.99 | $5 | $1.43 | −$2.32 |
| $14.99 | $8 | −$1.57 | −$5.32 |

A negative target CPA means the 15% target already fails with zero acquisition
cost. Under the same assumptions, a hypothetical $5 CPA requires **all-in
supplier cost at or below $12.67**, before the other unpriced costs. Both item
prices exceed that ceiling before shipping. The app's default $15 contribution
gate also fails even at zero shipping; that gate is a configurable rule, not
evidence of demand.

Calculation: `24.99 × (1 − .05 − .005 − .0519) − unit_cost − shipping
− .35 − .50 − (.005 × 9.44)`. Target CPA subtracts `24.99 × .15`.
The results were checked against `dropship.economics.UnitEconomics`.
