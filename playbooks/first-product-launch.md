## Sample acceptance notes

Record order date, dispatch date, first tracking update, arrival date and full
landed cost. Photograph the packaging and exact item. Film a continuous test on
your own furniture and note the fabric. Check collection, emptying, handle and
lid durability, and any fabric marking. Follow the supplier's instructions.
Do not generalize success on one fabric to every surface.

If the item damages fabric, differs from its description, or the supplier cannot
explain delivery and remedies clearly, resolve that before building the offer.

**Required product details:** Exact dimensions, materials, color/variant, package
contents, care instructions, compatible surfaces and limitations.

**Before the buy button:** Actual total price and currency, shipping charges,
eligible destinations, handling and delivery range, return window and return
postage terms, and monitored support email.

Price, delivery, guarantees and return terms are intentionally undecided. Replace
them with verified facts before producing the public page. Avoid invented reviews

## Three short video scripts

Film these with the actual sample. They are concepts, not performance claims.

| Concept | Opening | Demonstration | Close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sofa test | “Can this pick up the hair on this cushion?” | Show the untreated fabric, several passes in one continuous shot, then the result | “This is the result on our [fabric]. Details on the product page.” |
| Emptying | “Where does the hair go?” | Show a real pass, open the compartment, empty it and close it | “Here is what cleanup looks like.” |
| Honest limits | “We tried it on three fabrics.” | Label each surface and show results under consistent lighting | “Check the fabric guide before buying.” |

Do not disguise advertising as an independent customer review. Keep unsuccessful
tests in the assessment; they help determine whether the product is worth selling.

## First customer experiment

After the sample passes and checkout works, use one social channel and publish
the three demonstrations over one week. Record each post's visits to the product
page, checkout attempts where measurable, paid orders, support questions, refunds,
and actual costs. These observations guide the next change; three posts cannot
establish product-market fit.

Views alone do not justify ad spend. If no one visits, improve distribution and
the opening demonstration. If visits do not become purchases, check the price,
shipping, trust and checkout. If orders fail fulfillment, fix that before driving
more traffic. Choose any paid experiment only after the spending cap and actual
per-order economics are agreed.
