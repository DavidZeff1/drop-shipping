## Business registration

The Israel Tax Authority lists a 2026 expected annual turnover ceiling of
**₪122,833** for the online exempt-dealer route, with additional eligibility
conditions. This is turnover, not profit or startup capital. Check occupation,
existing activity and expected turnover before selecting עוסק פטור or עוסק מורשה.
The service opens VAT and income-tax files; required supporting documents include
identification and bank-account confirmation, with premises documents where relevant.
Use the official service for the exact checklist and submit private documents there.
[Official registration service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet).

National Insurance describes a combined registration process through the Tax
Authority and asks for expected work hours and income. Those figures must come
from the owner. Confirm that registration actually completed in both systems.
[National Insurance instructions](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/National%20Insurance/type_list/Self_Employed/Pages/howtoregister.aspx).

Do not treat “exempt dealer” as exemption from all taxes. Ask an Israeli tax
adviser to confirm income-tax, National Insurance, bookkeeping, receipt and VAT
treatment for the actual business, especially if goods move between foreign
countries. No tax classification or return has been filed in this session.

(permanent user code), with a link **הרשמה או הפקת קוד קבוע חדש** for registration
or a new code. Enter those privately on the official site. No identity information

## Payment route to verify

Start by checking an Israeli PayPal Business account. Confirm eligibility,
the available hosted checkout/payment-link product, physical-goods shipping-address
collection, supported currencies, card checkout experience, holds, bank withdrawals
and exports before connecting it. Do not assume every checkout feature is available
to every account. Stripe does not list Israel as a supported merchant country;
forming a foreign company solely for checkout is outside this initial plan.
[PayPal Israel](https://www.paypal.com/il/), [Stripe availability](https://stripe.com/global).

The [Israeli business page](https://www.paypal.com/il/business) lists Payment Links
and Buttons. After registration details are ready, use the
[official business-account signup page](https://www.paypal.com/il/business/open-business-account)
and complete the identity, bank and credential steps yourself. PayPal's
[Israeli agreement](https://www.paypal.com/il/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full)
requires individuals opening accounts to be at least 18 and resident in Israel.
This session has not established account eligibility or opened an account.

For the eventual verified product, the documented path is **Pay & Get Paid →
Create Payment Links and Buttons**. Use a fixed product price, the correct currency,
and the supported shipping-address collection settings. Test the actual address,
shipping charge, product identifier and confirmation workflow before publication.
[Official payment-link instructions](https://developer.paypal.com/payment-links-buttons/create-payment-link/).

PayPal's Israeli merchant fee page, updated 6 July 2026, lists domestic commercial
fees of 3.40% plus a fixed fee, or 4.01% plus a VAT-inclusive fixed fee. It lists
an additional 1.00% (1.18% including VAT) for US buyers and some other markets.
USD fixed fees are $0.30 or $0.35 including VAT. Thus a planning calculation for
a US buyer paying USD is **5.19% + $0.35 including VAT**, before currency conversion
and other applicable charges; this is an inference from the published table,
not a confirmed account quote. Conversion is separately listed at 2.5%, or 2.95%
including VAT, applied to the base exchange rate. Verify actual account treatment.
[Published merchant fees](https://www.paypal.com/il/business/paypal-business-fees?locale.x=en_IL).

Do not reuse the app's 2.9% + $0.30 defaults as Israeli verified fees. The current
engine also does not separately model sales VAT, customs or foreign exchange;
resolve those costs explicitly before relying on its profitability figures.

## Product and market decision

The pet-hair roller remains a candidate. Current research found a branded
ChomChom roller at **$24.99** on its seller's site. This is an advertised item
price, not a delivered-cost comparison; tax and shipping may differ. It challenges
the demo's $39.99 generic-product price. A higher price needs a demonstrated
customer benefit; no such advantage has been verified yet.
[Competitor listing](https://chomchomforpets.com/collections/rollers).

Israeli listings for reusable rollers also exist, so this is not an empty market.
Their fulfillment and current delivered totals have not been verified.
[Example Israeli listing](https://petsstore.co.il/products/roller).

Compare two routes before choosing: Hebrew sales into Israel with actual local
or imported delivery costs, and US sales with verified US fulfillment or a full
cross-border quote. For each, obtain product cost, shipping, duties/taxes, returns
address and cost, payment fees, and realistic delivery time. For commercial imports
into Israel, use the actual classification and check the
[official import requirements](https://apps.economy.gov.il/Apps/FreeImport/search).
Do not assume personal-import exemptions apply to commercial stock.

Choose the route that can deliver a credible offer with positive margin and a
workable returns process. If neither route works, reject the roller before buying
branding or ads. Supplier listings alone are not evidence of customer demand.
